Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone and your paired Apple Watch. Count Your Blessings has no account and no server.

How do I log a sneeze? Tap the big button on iPhone, tap anywhere on the watch, or use the Home Screen widget or Control Center control.

How do I undo a sneeze on the watch? Give your wrist a quick shake.

My sneezes aren't on the map. The map only shows sneezes logged while location access is allowed. Check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Count Your Blessings. Sneezes logged from widgets have no location.

The watch and phone show different counts. Open the app on both. They sync whenever the watch is connected to the phone.

How do I add sneezes I forgot to log? Tap ⋯ → Add Past Sneezes.

How do I get my data out? Tap ⋯ → Export as CSV.

How do I delete everything? Tap ⋯ → Reset → Reset All Data, or delete the app.
