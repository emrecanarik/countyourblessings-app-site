Count Your Blessings Privacy Policy

Last updated 19 October 2026

Count Your Blessings is built to collect nothing. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Data we collect

None. Count Your Blessings has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it has no server. We cannot see your sneezes or where they happened.

Data stored on your devices

Each sneeze is saved with its time, and with its location if you allow it. This is stored only on your iPhone, inside the app and its widgets, and on your paired Apple Watch. The phone and watch sync directly with each other using Apple's Watch Connectivity; nothing passes through our servers. Uninstalling the app removes this data. You can also erase it at any time with ⋯ → Reset → Reset All Data.

Location

The app asks for location access the first time you log a sneeze, so it can show your sneezes on a map. Location is used only while the app is open, is stored only on your devices, and is never sent to us or anyone else. You can say no, or turn it off later in Settings, and the app works the same apart from the map. The map itself is drawn by Apple Maps, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Exporting

Data leaves your device only when you choose to export it as a CSV file using the system share sheet. Where it goes from there is up to you and governed by whichever app or service you send it to.

Device backups

If you use iCloud Backup or a computer backup, your device's backup may include Count Your Blessings data under Apple's or your own control. We never have access to it.

Children

Count Your Blessings collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date.
